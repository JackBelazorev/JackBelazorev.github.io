1. Information We Collect

Our game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device ID (Advertising ID) — for ad personalization
  • IP address — for country-level location
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP, country level only)

Our game also uses Google Play Games Services for cloud saves and authentication.

2. How We Use Information

Data is used solely for displaying advertisements (AdMob) and cloud saves (Google Play Games Services). We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers.

3. Third-Party Services

4. Data Deletion

Users can reset their Advertising ID in Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID). All game data is stored locally and via Google Play Games cloud saves. Contact us to request data deletion.

5. In-Game Currency

All in-game currency is virtual and has no real monetary value. Virtual currency cannot be exchanged for real money. No real-money gambling occurs in this app.

6. Children's Privacy

This game is rated 12+ (PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 12.

7. Data Encryption
